# Traditional Food

Beyond cliché, these are where chilangos eat every day. 20 verified neighborhood taquerias and kitchens: giant trompos, 24h suadero vats, Guerrero green pozole, earth-pit barbacoa and tortas invented in 1892. \$ to \$\$, no markup.

**First 5 — real CSV extract (20 total):**

1. **El Vilsito** — Peten 248, Narvarte — `19.3897, -99.1578` — Mon-Thu 2pm-3am, Fri-Sat 2pm-5am — \$ 25-35 MXN — Auto shop by day, iconic pastor by night with giant spits. Local benchmark, pineapple sliced mid-air.
2. **El Gato Volador** — Eje 4 Xola 1301, Narvarte — `19.3970, -99.1504` — Mon-Sat 1pm-2am — \$ 22-30 MXN — Juicy pastor, suadero, bistec with beans and homemade salsas. Neighborhood favorite, spotless.
3. **Los Cocuyos** — Bolivar 57, Centro — `19.4317, -99.1384` — 24h — \$ 22-30 MXN — Suadero, tripa, cabeza confit 24h in steel vat. Bourdain's favorite; deep caramelized flavor.
4. **El Borrego Viudo** — Revolucion 241, Tacubaya — `19.3980, -99.1868` — 24h — \$ 20-28 MXN, tepache 25 — Historic drive-in carhop pastor/suadero + cold tepache. Ultimate democratic late-night institution.
5. **El Manantial** — Tamaulipas 72, Condesa — `19.4120, -99.1750` — Mon-Sat 11am-11pm — \$ 30-45 MXN — Unpretentious Condesa corner: costilla, chuleta, bistec con queso on coal.
