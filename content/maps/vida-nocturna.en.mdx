# Nightlife — Relax Bars

Want to drink and talk without shouting? 20 spots locals actually stay at: garrafon mezcalerias, clean pulquerias for first-timers, Porfirian cantinas with botana and sunset terraces. No list, no bouncer.

**How to use it (20 points):** Filter by plan: _mezcal_ (La Clandestina/Bosforo), _pulque_ (Los Insurgentes/La Nuclear), _cantina with botana_ (Corona/Opera/El Dux), _terrace_ (Ciudadela/Barrio). Most 6pm–2am; pulquerias from 2pm. Ask for seasonal curado or ancestral agave.

**First 5 — real CSV extract (20 total):**

1. **La Clandestina** — Alvaro Obregon 298, Condesa — `19.4162, -99.1704` — Tue-Sun 6pm-2am — \$\$ 130-190 MXN — Amber-lit mezcal den with wild agaves from demijohns, orange + worm salt. Staff guides without pretension.
2. **Bosforo** — Luis Moya 31, Centro — `19.4328, -99.1438` — Tue-Sat 7pm-2am — \$\$ 110-170 MXN — Moody candlelit mezcal hideaway near Alameda with rare ancestral agaves and eclectic jazz. Try hoja santa quesadillas.
3. **La Bipo (Condesa)** — Tamaulipas 5 — `19.4110, -99.1745` — Daily 1pm-2am — \$\$ \$60-90 beer, 120-160 mezcalita — Playful retro pop cantina, cold draft, terrace. Default chill spot in Condesa.
4. **Salon Tenampa** — Plaza Garibaldi 12 — `19.4402, -99.1392` — Daily 1pm-3am — \$\$ 80-160 MXN — Legendary 1925 cantina of Chavela Vargas; live mariachis at table. Singing rancheras with trumpets is rite.
5. **Salon Corona (Bolivar)** — Bolivar 24, Centro — `19.4338, -99.1388` — Daily 10am-11pm — \$ 45-75 MXN draft — Beer hall since 1928 with frosty mugs, roast beef tortas. Pure Centro folklore.
