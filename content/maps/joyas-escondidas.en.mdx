# Hidden Gems — 20 Places Not on TikTok

Small, quiet and curated. 20 spots locals save for when we want to breathe: floating libraries, lava temples, cloisters with coffee and colonial barrios frozen in time.

**How to use it (20 points):** Filter by mood: _architecture_ (Barragan/Gilardi), _quiet parks_ (Vasconcelos/Botanic Garden/Parque Espana), _intimate museums_ (Franz Mayer/MODO/MUPE). Most are free or under $5; Barragan and Gilardi require weeks-ahead booking.

**First 5 — real CSV extract (20 total):**

1. **Vasconcelos Library** — Eje 1 Norte Mosqueta S/N, Buenavista — `19.4457, -99.1451` — Daily 8:30am-7:30pm — Free — Alberto Kalach's floating steel shelves inside botanic garden with Gabriel Orozco whale skeleton. One of the most photogenic libraries worldwide.
2. **Morisco Kiosk Santa Maria** — Salvador Diaz Miron S/N — `19.4503, -99.1574` — Always open — Free — 1884 Moorish iron pavilion from New Orleans World's Fair in bohemian square. Sunday: artisan ice cream + danzon.
3. **Luis Barragan House** — Gral. Francisco Ramirez 12-14, Tacubaya — `19.4116, -99.1923` — By reservation only — \$\$\$ 450-550 MXN — Pritzker house-studio (UNESCO): pink walls, light shafts, poetic gardens. Sells out weeks ahead.
4. **Anahuacalli Museum** — Museo 150, Coyoacan South — `19.3125, -99.1372` — Wed-Sun 11am-5:30pm — \$\$ 100 MXN — Volcanic fortress-temple by Rivera for 50k pre-Hispanic pieces. Many locals rank it above Blue House; valley views from roof.
5. **Leon Trotsky House Museum** — Rio Churubusco 410, Coyoacan — `19.3548, -99.1494` — Tue-Sun 10am-5pm — \$\$ 90 MXN — Fortified house where Trotsky lived and was assassinated in 1940; intact desk and garden tomb. Quiet and evocative.
