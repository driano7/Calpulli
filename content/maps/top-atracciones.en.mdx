# Top Attractions — 40 Local-Curated Must-Sees

Not a TripAdvisor ranking. 40 places chilangos actually recommend because they keep curation, maintenance and sense of place — from the Sun Stone to the Floating Causeway.

**How to use this map (40 points):** Filter by area (Chapultepec / Centro / Coyoacan / South / Xochimilco-Teotihuacan) and by time: 2 days = Chapultepec + Centro; 4 days add Coyoacan-San Angel; full week close with Xochimilco and Teotihuacan at opening (8:00 to beat buses).

**First 5 — real CSV extract (40 total):**

1. **Museo Nacional de Antropologia** — Av. Paseo de la Reforma S/N, Chapultepec — `19.4260, -99.1863` — Tue-Sun 9am-6pm — \$\$ 95 MXN (Sundays free for nationals) — Mexico's crowning museum by Pedro Ramirez Vazquez, housing the Aztec Sun Stone, colossal Olmec heads and Pakal's tomb.
2. **Chapultepec Castle & National History Museum** — Chapultepec — `19.4204, -99.1819` — Tue-Sun 9am-5pm — \$\$ 95 MXN — The only royal castle in the Americas atop the hill; panoramic terraces to Reforma and Maximilian & Carlota salons. Go at golden hour.
3. **Chapultepec Forest (Mayor Lake & Floating Causeway)** — Sect I & II — `19.4177, -99.1822` — Free — One of the largest urban parks in the Americas; boating lakes, trails and the award-winning Floating Causeway through the canopy.
4. **Soumaya Museum (Plaza Carso)** — Blvd. Cervantes Saavedra 303, Polanco — `19.4406, -99.2047` — Daily 10:30am-6:30pm — Free — Fernando Romero's silver hourglass with the largest private Rodin collection outside France.
5. **Tamayo Contemporary Art Museum** — Reforma 51, Chapultepec — `19.4261, -99.1819` — Tue-Sun 10am-6pm — \$\$ 85 MXN — Brutalist museum integrated into the forest + brunch terrace among trees.

> **Local tip:** Book Anthropology and Blue House online (sell out), arrive 15 min early, avoid Sundays if you dislike crowds. For Teotihuacan (point 39) arrive at 8:00.
