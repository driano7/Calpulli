# Areas to Stay Alert — Data, not fear

Safety isn't rumor, it's data. This map gathers 8 zones where you should be extra careful by time and context, based on official stats and local judgment. They are not “no-go zones”, they're perimeters where carelessness costs.

**How to read it:** Each point shows level (High / Medium-High / Medium), borough, highest-risk time window and most common type (pickpocketing, mugging). By day and on main avenues most are walkable; at night use rideshare and request it at the door.

**The 8 zones (first 5 extract):**

1. **Tepito (Eje 1 Norte informal commerce)** — `19.4460, -99.1268` — Cuauhtemoc — **High — Avoid at night; by day only with local contact** — Risk spikes after 5pm when stalls close. Dense informal trade, pickpocketing and turf disputes. Cited in all consular advisories.
2. **La Merced Market & alleys** — `19.4258, -99.1213` — Venustiano Carranza — **Medium-High — Day with caution; avoid at night** — Bustling 7am–2pm, deserted alleys at night. Phone/chain theft. Go early, leave by metro/rideshare.
3. **Doctores (inner streets at night)** — `19.4160, -99.1470` — Cuauhtemoc — **Medium-High at night — Day on avenues ok** — Arena Mexico Tue/Fri fine by day; inner streets deserted at night. For lucha libre, Uber door-to-door.
4. **Iztapalapa deep east residential** — `19.3467, -99.0500` — Iztapalapa — **High in far east — Central parks safe by day** — Violent crime concentrates in non-tourist colonias. Cerro de la Estrella park safe by day; no reason to go deep east.
5. **Nezahualcoyotl eastern fringe** — `19.4003, -99.0148` — EdoMex border — **Medium-High — Non-tourist conurbation** — No tourist corridors. Keep to consolidated corridors (Centro-Roma-Condesa-Coyoacan).

**+3 complete the map (see My Map):**

- **Ecatepec north fringe** — High — Not recommended; for Teotihuacan use authorized tour or direct bus from North Terminal.
- **Centro Historico — Perimeter B alleys at night** — Medium at night — Madero/Regina policed; avoid dark alleys after 10:30pm.
- **Metro network (Line B & 3 rush hour)** — Medium — Petty theft, not violence — 7–9:30am & 6–8:30pm. Backpack on chest, phone away.
