## Qué hacer

Browse through the dusty shelves of El Laberinto bookstore on Calle Álvaro Obregón. Look for mid-century Mexican poetry editions and old maps of the Federal District tucked away in the back corners.

## Qué comer

Order a cortado and a sourdough croissant at Erre & Co on Calle Orizaba.

## Entender a los mexicanos

Book hunting in old neighbourhoods is an exercise in tactile nostalgia; people here still value physical paper as proof of memory.

## Para qué nos sirve en Amaxing

It reminds us that the Roma is built on stories, paper, and intellectual conversations rather than just cafes.
