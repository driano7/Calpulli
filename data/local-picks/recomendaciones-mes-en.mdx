# Activities of the Month in CDMX

What to do this month in Mexico City, recommended by locals.

## What's happening this month

There's always something happening in the city. From markets and fairs to exhibitions and concerts, every neighborhood has something interesting to discover.

## Recommendations

- **Centro Histórico** – Walk around the Zócalo, visit Palacio de Bellas Artes, and stroll the pedestrian streets. Great for a family Sunday.
- **Roma Norte** – Specialty coffee shops, independent bookstores, and art galleries. Perfect for wandering without a map and discovering hidden corners.
- **Coyoacán** – The handicrafts market, Casa Azul, and the gardens. A quiet walk with traditional flavor.
- **Polanco** – Museo Jumex, Museo Soumaya, and the stores on Presidente Masaryk Avenue. Great for a day of shopping and culture.
- **Condesa** – Parque México, Parque España, and the restaurants on Avenida Ámsterdam. Great for ending the day.

> Tip: Museums usually have free admission on Sundays. Check opening hours before you go.
