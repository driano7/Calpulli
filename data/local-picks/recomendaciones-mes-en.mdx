# Activities of the Month in CDMX — November 2026

November in Mexico City brings Day of the Dead, the Revolution anniversary and perfect weather for walking. Here are 8 activities with date, location and price, recommended by locals.

1. Paseo Dominical en Bicicleta (Free / Family-friendly / Weekend)

- When: Every Sunday, 8:00 AM – 2:00 PM.
- Location: Reforma and Centro Histórico.
- Description: The city closes major avenues to cars so you can bike, skate, or walk freely.
- Price: Free (Bring your own bike or use the local Ecobici system).
- Tip: Grab a _torta de chilaquiles_ from a street cart near the Monumento a la Revolución when you need a carb reload.

2. Museo Nacional de Antropología (Family-friendly / Weekend)

- When: Tuesday to Sunday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
- Location: Bosque de Chapultepec.
- Description: World-class museum housing massive pre-Hispanic artifacts, including the famous Aztec Sun Stone.
- Price: $ (Approx. $95 MXN; free for Mexican nationals and foreign residents on Sundays).
- Tip: Head straight to the Mexica room first to avoid the afternoon crowds, then walk through the park.

3. Mercado Roma (Weekend / Foodie)

- When: Monday to Sunday, 11:00 AM – midnight.
- Location: Roma Norte.
- Description: A chic, multi-level upscale food hall featuring local culinary concepts, craft beers, and gourmet bites.
- Price: $$ (Varies by stall, expect $200–$400 MXN per person).
- Tip: Go for early dinner on Saturday to secure a rooftop table overlooking Orizaba street.

4. Cineteca Nacional (Weekend / Evening)

- When: Tuesday to Sunday, check daily showtimes (usually from 12:00 PM onwards).
- Location: Xoco (Coyoacán area).
- Description: Mexico’s premier film institute showing independent, international, and classic Mexican cinema under open-air and indoor screens.
- Price: $ (Tickets around $70–$90 MXN).
- Tip: Grab an artisan ice cream in the central courtyard before your screening.

5. Bosque de Tlalpan (Free / Nature / Weekend)

- When: Monday to Sunday, 5:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
- Location: Tlalpan.
- Description: A protected natural forest area in the southern part of the city, perfect for a trail run or a quiet morning walk among native oak trees.
- Price: Free.
- Tip: Stop by the historic center of Tlalpan afterward for traditional _café de olla_ and sweet bread at a local bakery.

6. Palacio de Bellas Artes (Culture / Weekend)

- When: Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
- Location: Centro Histórico.
- Description: Marvel at the stunning white marble architecture and iconic muralism by Rivera, Siqueiros, and Orozco inside.
- Price: $ (Approx. $90 MXN; free on Sundays).
- Tip: Walk up to the Sears café across the street for the best panoramic view of the palace's orange-domed roof.

7. Mercado de San Juan (Foodie / Local Experience)

- When: Monday to Sunday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
- Location: Centro Histórico (Puerta 2).
- Description: A legendary market known for exotic foods, gourmet cheeses, tapas bars, and fresh local produce.
- Price: $$ (Depending on whether you just snack or sit down for a glass of wine and Iberian ham).
- Tip: Try a shot of artisanal mezcal paired with chapulines (grasshoppers) at one of the dedicated tasting stalls.

8. Jardín Botánico del Bosque de Chapultepec (Free / Family-friendly)

- When: Tuesday to Sunday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
- Location: First Section of Chapultepec.
- Description: A peaceful sanctuary featuring hundreds of native cacti, orchids, and medicinal plants right in the middle of the urban chaos.
- Price: Free.
- Tip: Combine it with a stroll around the nearby Lago Mayor for a complete low-cost afternoon out.

> **General tip:** Check schedules and prices before heading out, as they can change without notice. Many museums offer free admission on Sundays for nationals.
