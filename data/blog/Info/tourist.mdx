## Tips for visitors

- Download the **Mi Policía** app before traveling — it has an emergency button and WhatsApp assistance line +52 55 4891 1116.
- Save emergency numbers: **911** (emergencies), **\*0311** (Locatel), **+52 55 4891 1116** (Tourist Police WhatsApp).
- The **Seismic Alert** warns seconds before an earthquake — install the **SkyAlert** app.
